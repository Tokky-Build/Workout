# Shopee Affiliate x AI YouTube channel (working name: "AI Desk Setup TH")

- What it is: a faceless YouTube channel (long videos plus Shorts) in Thai, produced end to end with AI (AI voice, AI/stock visuals, AI-assisted scripts), that earns money from Shopee Affiliate commission on products linked in the video description and pinned comment, and later from YouTube ads once the channel is monetised.
- Who it is for: Thai viewers aged about 20-35 who work from a desk or study (office workers, students, freelancers) and search for gadgets, desk-setup items and small office/IT accessories before buying on Shopee.
- What it sells, at what price: no product of its own. It sells viewers' purchase decisions: the viewer buys items priced roughly 100-2,000 THB on Shopee and the channel earns a commission. The commission rate per category is NOT known yet (open question; check the current Shopee Affiliate rate card). YouTube ad revenue starts only after Partner Program thresholds.
- Where and how: online only. Viewers find videos through YouTube search, recommendations and Shorts. Delivery is a video plus links in the description. Content format is decided by the founder: AI voice plus stock/AI visuals, no face on camera.
- Budget and constraints: 5,000+ THB per month and serious, regular effort. One person. Skills: not stated. Category was chosen by the assistant (not by the founder) as the easiest to make without appearing on camera; the founder asked the assistant to decide from their work, age and free time, which the assistant does not know. Treat the category as an assumption to be tested, not a decision. Rules to respect: disclose affiliate links, disclose realistic AI-generated or synthetic content where the platform requires, no exaggerated product claims.
