# Two-track AI short-video business (working name: "Everyday Essentials TH" + comedy shorts)

- What it is: one founder makes faceless, AI-assisted short videos in Thai on two separate tracks. Track A: short clips about cheap everyday essentials (for example portable bidet sprayers, travel and bathroom items) on TikTok, Facebook Reels and Instagram Reels, earning Shopee Affiliate commission through the cart/link. Track B: short comedy clips on YouTube (Shorts), earning YouTube ad revenue only, with no Shopee links.
- Who it is for: Track A: Thai mobile viewers who want practical, low-priced daily-use items and decide quickly. Track B: Thai YouTube Shorts viewers who watch comedy. Exact age and segment are not defined yet.
- What it sells, at what price: Track A sells viewers' purchase decisions on items priced low (roughly tens to a few hundred THB; exact prices unknown) and earns a commission whose rate is unknown (to be read from the founder's own Shopee Affiliate account). Track B sells nothing; revenue is YouTube ads after Partner Program thresholds.
- Where and how: online only, Thailand. Viewers find clips through the platform feeds. Production is AI voice and AI or stock visuals, no face on camera, based on summarising real buyer reviews (the founder is abroad and cannot test products). The founder returns to Thailand to open the Shopee account and research affiliate rules.
- Budget and constraints: 5,000+ THB per month, serious effort, one person, skills not stated. Cannot handle products. Product category on Track A was proposed by the founder (everyday essentials, portable bidet) and is not validated. Rules to respect: disclose affiliate links and AI use, no exaggerated hygiene or safety claims, check each platform's rules for cart/link use, and YouTube's rule against repetitive mass-produced AI content (comedy must have original jokes and perspective).
