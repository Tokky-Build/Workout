# Brief for the board

Judge the business below. You cannot see the conversation that produced it; everything you need is here. Do not invent market sizes, commission rates or competitor facts: name them as open questions.

## The business
- A faceless Thai-language YouTube channel (long videos plus Shorts) made end to end with AI: AI voice, AI or stock visuals, AI-assisted scripts. No presenter on camera.
- Niche (assumed, not validated): gadgets, desk-setup and small office/IT accessories for Thai viewers aged about 20-35 (office workers, students, freelancers).
- Revenue: Shopee Affiliate commission on products linked in descriptions and pinned comments (viewers buy items priced roughly 100-2,000 THB); later YouTube Partner Program ads once the channel qualifies. Commission rate per category: unknown.
- Founder: one person, working in a serious regular schedule, budget 5,000+ THB per month, skills not stated.
- Market: Thailand, online only. Platforms: YouTube plus Shopee.

## Known constraints and facts
- YouTube may reduce or refuse monetisation for repetitive, mass-produced or low-effort AI content; channels need original value.
- YouTube Partner Program needs subscriber and watch-time (or Shorts views) thresholds; affiliate links work before that.
- Affiliate links must be disclosed; product claims must not be exaggerated; realistic synthetic media may need a disclosure.
- Shopee can change commission rates and rules at any time.
- Thai affiliate and review content on YouTube, TikTok and Facebook is crowded.

## What the founder wants to know
Can this earn real money, and what would have to be true for it to work?
