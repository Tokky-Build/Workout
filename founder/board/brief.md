# Brief for the board

Judge the business below. You cannot see the conversation that produced it; everything you need is here. Do not invent market sizes, commission rates or competitor facts: name them as open questions.

## The business
Two tracks run by one person, faceless, Thai language, AI-assisted production (AI voice, AI or stock visuals):
- Track A (income): short clips on TikTok, Facebook Reels and Instagram Reels about cheap everyday essentials (for example portable bidet sprayers, travel and bathroom items). Revenue: Shopee Affiliate commission via cart/link. Commission rate and item prices: unknown.
- Track B: short comedy clips on YouTube Shorts. Revenue: YouTube ads only, after the Partner Program thresholds. No Shopee links on YouTube.
- Founder: one person, budget 5,000+ THB per month, serious effort, skills not stated. Currently abroad, cannot test products physically, so product videos would summarise real buyer reviews and say so. Will return to Thailand to open the Shopee Affiliate account and research the rules and rates.
- Market: Thailand, online only.

## Known constraints and facts
- YouTube may demonetise repetitive or mass-produced AI content (policy "inauthentic content", effective 15 July 2025, with later tightening reported in 2026 by secondary sources); the penalty can apply to the whole channel.
- YouTube Partner Program needs subscriber and watch-time or Shorts-view thresholds; Track A links work before any threshold on the platforms that allow them.
- Affiliate links must be disclosed; product claims, especially hygiene or health, must not be exaggerated; platform rules for cart or link use differ and have not been checked.
- Shopee affiliate rates are unknown and can change at any time; sources found online conflict (some say up to 25.9%, others 5-10%; none official).
- Low-priced items mean low commission per sale. Thai affiliate and short-video content is crowded, especially on TikTok.
- Competitor research was not completed (see founder/competitors.md, marked incomplete).
- A previous board vote on a single-track gadget channel was FUND IF at an average of 3.7/10; this brief is a changed business and should be judged fresh.
