# Board summary: two-track AI short-video business

## Vote
3 x FUND IF, 0 x FUND, 0 x PASS. Scores: Offers 3, Monopoly 3, Product 4. Average 3.3 / 10.
Lower than the earlier single-channel version (3.7). "Fund if" means: only a small, time-boxed test of Track A is justified until the conditions below are met.

## Risks more than one member raised (most important first)
1. **Track B (YouTube comedy) is the weakest part.** All three say it splits one person's effort, has no offer (ads only, after thresholds), and a repetitive AI comedy channel fits the profile YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule targets. (Offers, Monopoly, Product)
2. **Track A is a commodity.** Cheap essentials, low commission per sale, crowded Thai short-video affiliate content, nothing yet that makes a viewer pick this creator over the next one. Anyone can copy AI voice plus review summaries in month six. (Offers, Monopoly, Product)
3. **Unknown economics.** Shopee commission rate and item prices are unknown; sources conflict and none is official. At low item prices the commission per sale may not justify the work. (Offers, Monopoly, Product)
4. **Rented distribution.** Reach depends on TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube algorithms; pay depends on Shopee's rates and rules. No owned audience. (Monopoly, Product, Offers)
5. **Weak proof.** The founder cannot handle products and the channel is faceless, so buyers only get summarised reviews. Hygiene and health claims add compliance risk. (Offers, Product)
6. **Unchecked platform rules** for carts and links on TikTok, Facebook and Instagram. (Offers, Product)

## Conditions (checklist for the rest of the pack)
- [ ] Run Track A alone first; park Track B until Track A earns a sale and has a clear human point of view. (all three)
- [ ] Narrow Track A to one specific niche and one-sentence promise, with one fixed clip format and a quality checklist. (Monopoly, Product, Offers) -> /founder-competitors, /founder-offer
- [ ] Find one angle that is not "cheaper": a buying guide, comparison or "which one to buy" pick, with the review source shown. (Offers, Product)
- [ ] Per-item routine: reviews read, price and stock confirmed the same day, no health or hygiene exaggeration, affiliate and AI disclosure on every clip. (Offers, Product)
- [ ] Get the real Shopee rate, item prices and platform link rules in Thailand; /founder-cfo must show break-even clicks and sales for the 5,000 THB budget. Stop if the numbers do not clear. (all three) -> /founder-cfo
- [ ] Finish competitor research; run the buyer panel on the pitch. (all three) -> /founder-competitors, /founder-consumer
- [ ] Time-boxed test with a stated kill number (for example sales per 1,000 views). (Monopoly, Offers) -> /founder-launch
- [ ] Build one owned channel (group or list) from week one. (Monopoly)

## Where the members disagree
- Product (4) is the most positive: it likes the short clip-to-purchase path and the honest review-summary approach, and would simply cut Track B for now.
- Offers and Monopoly (3 each) see no differentiated offer or moat; Monopoly adds that Track B could taint the founder's other accounts if linked.

## The strongest version the board can see
Track A only: one narrow niche of everyday essentials, a "which one to buy" format built from real buyer reviews with sources shown, a per-item check routine and clear disclosure, plus a small owned community. Run as a time-boxed test with a kill number. Track B (YouTube comedy) waits until Track A earns, and only returns with a real human point of view. This differs from the two-track pitch, and the board says so plainly.
