# Board summary: Shopee Affiliate x AI YouTube channel

## Vote
3 x FUND IF, 0 x FUND, 0 x PASS. Scores: Offers 4, Monopoly 3, Product 4. Average 3.7 / 10.
This is a low score. "Fund if" here means: only a small, cheap test is justified until the conditions below are met.

## Risks more than one member raised (most important first)
1. **No reason to choose this channel.** Thai gadget review and affiliate content is crowded; the brief describes a production method (AI, faceless), not an advantage or an experience. (Offers, Monopoly, Product)
2. **No proof the product was ever used.** AI voice, stock visuals and AI scripts give viewers no evidence, and trust is what makes a link click. (Offers, Product)
3. **Platform dependence.** YouTube may refuse or cut monetisation for mass-produced AI content, and Shopee can change commission and rules at any time; the founder owns neither audience, traffic nor revenue source. (Offers, Monopoly, Product)
4. **Unknown economics.** The commission rate per category, the click and purchase rates, and revenue per 1,000 views are all unknown. Tickets are small (about 100-2,000 THB). (Offers, Monopoly, Product)
5. **Broad, assumed niche.** The category was chosen by the assistant, not validated. (Offers, Monopoly)

## Conditions (checklist for the rest of the pack)
- [ ] Pick one narrow sub-niche and one-sentence promise before making more than a small batch. (Monopoly, Product) -> /founder-competitors, /founder-consumer
- [ ] Show an open gap against existing Thai channels. (Offers, Monopoly) -> /founder-competitors
- [ ] Confirm a named viewer segment with real pain and real buy intent. (Offers, Product) -> /founder-consumer
- [ ] Each video carries original value: real product footage or photos, hands-on testing, honest cons, comparisons. No purely synthetic review claims. (all three) -> /founder-offer
- [ ] Disclose affiliate links and AI use in every video and description; keep claims honest. (Offers, Product)
- [ ] CFO model with the real commission rate that still works at a lower commission and with zero YouTube ad income. (Offers, Monopoly, Product) -> /founder-cfo
- [ ] Cheap real test first: about 10 videos with measured views, link clicks and Shopee orders before scaling spend. (all three) -> /founder-launch
- [ ] Stop if the numbers do not work. (Product)

## Where the members disagree
- Monopoly scored lowest (3): it sees no durable advantage at all, since AI tools are available to everyone and a copycat can match the channel in weeks.
- Offers and Product (4 each) see more upside in the cheap test and the short video-to-purchase path, but both say the faceless AI-only format is the weakest part.

## The strongest version the board can see
Not "a faceless AI gadget channel", but one narrow sub-niche with one clear promise (for example, only products that were actually tested, with real photos or footage, honest pros and cons, and local Shopee price checks), using AI to speed up scripting, editing and voice while a human supplies the proof. That differs from what was pitched (fully AI, no real product contact), and the board says plainly that the fully-AI version is the one most exposed to the risks above.
